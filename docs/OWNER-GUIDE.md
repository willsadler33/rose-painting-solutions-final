# Your website manager — a quick guide

Your website has a private **website manager** where you can add photos, create before & after sliders, and change the words and details on your site. It works on your phone and on a computer. You don't need any technical knowledge, and nothing you do here can break the design of your website.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **your website address followed by `/admin`** (for example `www.yourwebsite.com/admin`).
2. Type your password and tap **Sign in**.
3. **The first time only:** you'll be asked to choose your own password. Use at least 10 characters — a short sentence you'll remember, like `Blue walls in the hallway 2026`.

> **Tip:** On your phone, add the page to your home screen (Safari: Share › Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app.

You stay signed in on that device for up to 2 weeks (or until 3 days pass without you using it). To sign out, tap **More › Sign out** on a phone, or **Sign out** at the bottom left on a computer.

**Forgotten your password?** Contact your website developer — they can reset it for you.

## Changing your password

**Account & security › Change your password.** Type your current password, then the new one twice. Changing it signs out any other phone or computer you were signed in on.

---

## The two most important ideas

**1. Nothing goes on your website until you publish.**
Everything you change is saved straight away as a *draft*. When you've made changes, an **amber bar** appears at the top saying *Unpublished changes*:

- **Preview** opens your website showing your changes — only you can see this.
- **Publish** puts all your changes on the website for everyone. They appear within a minute.

**2. Every photo and project has a label:**

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Live** (blue) | Shown on your website (after you publish) |
| **Draft** (yellow) | Not on your website yet |
| **Hidden** (grey) | Taken off your website, but kept so you can bring it back |

## Adding a new before & after project (the 2-minute version)

Perfect for the end of a job, straight from your phone:

1. Tap **Projects**, then **Create before & after** (or use the big blue button on the home screen).
2. **Before photo:** tap *Choose a photo* and pick the photo from before you started.
3. **After photo:** tap *Choose a photo* and pick the finished photo.
   *Take both from roughly the same spot if you can — it makes the slider look its best.*
4. Type a **project title** (e.g. *Living room repaint*). Add a short line and a description if you like.
5. Under **Framing & preview**, drag the bright window on each photo to choose which part shows, and use **Zoom** to line the two photos up. Switch between **Computer** and **Phone** to check both. Drag the slider in the preview to try it.
6. Tap **Publish now** — or **Save as draft** if you want to finish it later.

The photos are labelled **BEFORE** and **AFTER** at every step. If you put them the wrong way round, tap **Swap before and after**.

> **Location:** keep it general (a town or area) — never a customer's address.

## Editing a project

**Projects › Edit** on the project. You can change the photos, the text and the framing, then **Save changes** and publish.

- **Hide it:** tap **Hidden** on the project. It comes off your website when you publish, but stays here.
- **Delete it:** tap the bin icon. It goes to the **Bin** at the bottom of the page, where you can **Restore** it or **Delete for good**.
- **Change the order:** drag the projects (on a computer), or use the **↑ ↓** arrows. The first project is the one visitors see first.

## Uploading photos

**Media library › Choose photos** (or drag photos onto the box on a computer). You can pick several at once.

- Photos are made web-ready on your phone or computer before they upload: resized, saved in a fast format, and **location data removed** for privacy.
- A progress bar shows each upload. On a slow connection, just wait — it retries automatically.
- iPhone photos work as normal.
- If a file isn't a photo, you'll see a message and nothing is uploaded.

## The photo gallery ("Finished rooms")

**Gallery** manages the strip of finished-job photos under your before & after projects.

- **Add photos:** upload new ones (they arrive as **Draft**), or **Add from media library**.
- **Show it:** tap **Live** on the photo, then publish.
- **Change the order:** drag the photos, or use the **↑ ↓** arrows.
- **Remove from gallery:** the ✕ button. The photo stays in your media library.

## Changing a photo's title or description

Tap **Edit** on any photo (in the gallery, the media library or a project).

- **Photo title** — shown with the photo, e.g. *Complete Living Room Repaint — Bright Modern Finish*.
- **Short description** — shown when someone opens the photo full screen.
- **Description for screen readers** — describe what's in the photo for visitors who can't see it.

Tap **Save photo**, then publish.

## Cropping, rotating and adjusting a photo

**Edit › Crop & adjust:**

- **Rotate** turns the photo a quarter turn.
- Drag the **crop box** or its corners, or pick a shape (4:3, square…).
- **Brightness** and **Contrast** allow small corrections only, so photos of your work stay true to life.
- **Reset edits** takes the photo back to how you uploaded it. The original is always kept, so editing never lowers the quality.

**Edit › Focus point:** tap the most important part of the photo. When the photo is shown in a different shape, that spot stays in view.

## Replacing a photo

**Edit › Details › Replace photo.** The new photo appears everywhere the old one was used, keeping its title and description.

## Hiding or deleting photos

In the **Media library**, tick photos and choose **Move to bin**. Photos in the bin can be **restored**, or **deleted for good** (they're removed the next time you publish).

> A photo that's part of a before & after project can't be put in the bin — change the project's photo first.

## Changing the words on your website

**Text** (Website text) lets you change the headline, the text under it, button wording, section headings and more. A preview next to each box shows how it will look. The small counter stops the text getting too long for the design. **Restore original wording** puts back the original text. Tap **Save text**, then publish.

## Services

**More › Services.** Edit a service's name, description, what's included and its colour; **switch off** services you no longer offer (they stay saved); **Add a service**; and drag or use the arrows to change the order.

## Business details and opening hours

**More › Business settings.** Change your business name, phone number, email, the areas you cover, a note for customers, opening days and times, and social media links. The website shows the hours wording automatically (e.g. *Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM*).

> Changing the email shown on the website doesn't change where enquiry-form messages are sent. Ask your developer if you need that changed.

---

## Made a mistake?

- **Before publishing:** **Account & security › Undo unpublished changes** puts everything back to what's on your website now.
- **After publishing:** **Account & security › Earlier versions of your website** lists every published version (including the original website). **Bring back** one, check it with **Preview**, then **Publish**.
- **Deleted something?** Check the **Bin** in the media library and on the projects page.

## Need help?

Contact your website developer. Tell them what you were trying to do and, if you saw a message, what it said.
